About

The Story Behind Everything.

I never set out to build multiple businesses. I was simply trying to build a life that felt like my own.

Becoming a mother at a young age, rebuilding after divorce and starting over more than once taught me that reinvention rarely comes with a roadmap. Every challenge shaped the way I think, the way I lead and the opportunities I chose to pursue.

Today, every venture I've built has started with a real problem worth solving. This website isn't a portfolio or a place to promote my businesses. It's a collection of the journey behind them, the ideas that continue to shape them and the lessons I'm still learning along the way.
